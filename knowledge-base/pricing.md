## 3. Price Match Guarantee

ComputeIQ Tech guarantees competitive pricing:
- If you discover an identical, brand-new, factory-sealed product offered at a lower price by an authorized major retailer (e.g., Best Buy, B&H Photo, Amazon direct sales), we will match the price.
- **Window**: Applicable before purchase or within **14 calendar days** following purchase.
- **Exclusions**: Refurbished/open-box stock, marketplace third-party sellers, auction sites, clearance liquidations, and Black Friday doorbusters.
