# ComputeIQ Tech — Return & Refund Policy

At ComputeIQ Tech, customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, review our transparent return and refund policies below.

---

## 1. 7-Day Standard Return Window

Customers have **7 calendar days** from the date of physical delivery (or in-store purchase) to initiate a return for an exchange or full refund.

### Return Conditions
To qualify for a full refund or exchange:
1. **Pristine Condition**: The item must be unused, undamaged, and free from any cosmetic scratches, marks, or personal data.
2. **Complete Original Packaging**: The product must be returned with its original manufacturer box, intact serial number barcodes, foam inserts, manuals, power adapters, and bundled cables.
3. **Proof of Purchase**: The customer must present the physical register receipt, invoice, or online order confirmation number.

---

## 2. Non-Returnable & Hygiene-Restricted Items

Due to hygiene, safety, and health regulations:
- **NovaBuds Earbuds (In-Ear)**: Once the sanitary factory box seal has been broken or opened, NovaBuds **cannot be returned for a refund**, unless the unit is verified to be DOA (Dead On Arrival) or has a verified hardware defect.
- **Gift Cards & Software**: Downloadable software licenses, digital redemption codes, and ComputeIQ gift cards are strictly non-refundable.
- **Clearance Items**: Any items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" cannot be returned.

---

## 3. Damaged in Transit or Defective Upon Arrival (DOA)

- If your parcel arrives visibly crushed, opened, or if the product does not power on right out of the box, you must report it within **48 hours of delivery**.
- Send photos of the shipping box, product packaging, and defect to **support@computeiq.tech**.
- ComputeIQ Tech will immediately dispatch a replacement unit and issue a prepaid courier pickup label at zero expense to you.

---

## 4. Restocking Fees

- **Zero Restocking Fee**: Unopened, sealed items returned within 7 days incur **no restocking fee**.
- **15% Open-Box Fee**: If a laptop (NovaBook Air / Pro) has been opened, booted, and tested, but is in immaculate physical condition with all accessories, a 15% open-box restocking fee applies to cover hardware sanitization and testing.

---

## 5. Refund Processing Times

- Once your return arrives at our Manhattan facility, our technical team completes inspection within **2 business days**.
- Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment method:
  - **Credit/Debit Cards**: 3 to 5 business days (depending on your issuing bank).
  - **Apple Pay / Google Pay**: 1 to 3 business days.
  - **In-Store Cash Purchases**: Instant cash or original debit card refund at the counter.
