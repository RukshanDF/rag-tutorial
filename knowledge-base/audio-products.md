## 1. NovaBuds True Wireless Earbuds

- **Product Category**: Audio & Wearables
- **Model Number**: NB-500-TWS
- **Price**: LKR 12,500 (Approx. $42 USD)
- **Warranty**: 6-Month Limited Warranty
- **Available Colors**: Arctic White, Matte Obsidian, Sky Blue

### Sound & Acoustic Performance
- **Drivers**: 11mm custom dynamic drivers engineered with rigid ultra-thin graphene diaphragms for punchy bass and crystalline highs.
- **Frequency Response**: 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz.
- **Audio Codecs**: AAC and SBC supported over high-bandwidth Bluetooth.
- **Sound Profile**: Tuned for balanced studio clarity, with customizable EQ presets available via quick tap sequences.

### Noise Cancellation & Ambient Modes
- **Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)**: Dual internal and external microphones suppress up to 38dB of low-frequency ambient environmental noise (airplane cabin roar, subway rumble, office chatter).
- **Transparency Mode**: One-touch pass-through mode channels external human voices and ambient sound so you can cross city streets or converse without removing the earbuds.

### Battery & Charging
- **Earbud Playback Time**:
  - Up to 7 hours of continuous listening (ANC OFF).
  - Up to 6 hours of continuous listening (ANC ON).
- **Total Playback with Charging Case**: Up to 30 hours total.
- **Quick Charge Feature**: 10 minutes in the charging case provides 2 full hours of listening time.
- **Case Charging Interface**: USB-C fast charging port (charging cable included) and Qi wireless charging pad compatibility.

### Connectivity & Smart Features
- **Bluetooth Version**: Bluetooth 5.4 with low-latency gaming mode (sub-60ms delay).
- **Bluetooth Multipoint**: Connects to 2 devices simultaneously (e.g., laptop and smartphone) with automatic audio switching.
- **Call Quality**: 4 environmental beamforming microphones equipped with AI deep neural network (DNN) wind-noise reduction.
- **Water & Sweat Resistance**: IPX5 rating (sweatproof for intense workouts, running, and light rain).
- **Touch Controls**: Capacitive sensor on both stems for play/pause, track skip, volume control, ANC toggle, and Siri/Google Assistant activation.
