## 5. Shipping & Order Fulfillment Policies

### Local New York City (NYC 5 Boroughs) Delivery
- **Courier Rate**: **$15 USD** (or LKR 500 equivalent for international demo orders).
- **Free Delivery**: Available on all orders over **$500 USD** (or LKR 50,000 equivalent).
- **Delivery Timeline**: 1 to 2 business days via dedicated NYC courier service.

### US Domestic Shipping (Outside NYC)
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: **$35 USD** (or LKR 1,200 equivalent).
- **Carriers**: Shipped via insured FedEx Ground or UPS with tracking provided upon dispatch.
- **Delivery Timeline**: 2 to 4 business days.
- **Express Overnight**: Available at checkout for an additional $25 fee (delivery by 10:30 AM next business day).

### In-Store Order Pickup (Click & Collect)
- **Cost**: **100% Free** with no minimum order value.
- **Preparation Time**: Ready within 2 hours for in-stock products.
- **Pickup Requirements**: Bring an official government photo ID and your order barcode confirmation email.
