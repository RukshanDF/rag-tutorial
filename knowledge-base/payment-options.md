# ComputeIQ Tech — Payment Options & Financing

ComputeIQ Tech offers multiple secure, fast, and flexible payment methods for both online orders and in-store purchases at our Manhattan showroom.

---

## 1. Supported Payment Methods

### Credit & Debit Cards
We accept all major international and domestic cards:
- **Visa** (Credit & Debit)
- **MasterCard** (Credit & Debit)
- **American Express** (Amex)
- **Discover**
- **JCB** & **UnionPay**

### Digital Wallets & Contactless Pay
- **Apple Pay**: Supported on web (Safari/iOS) and via NFC terminals in-store.
- **Google Pay**: Supported on Chrome/Android and in-store tap-to-pay.
- **PayPal**: Available for instant checkout on online purchases.

### Direct Bank Transfers (ACH / Wire)
- Accepted for corporate, educational, and high-volume wholesale orders above $1,000 USD (or LKR 300,000).
- Invoices include official routing/SWIFT information with payment terms net-15 or net-30 upon credit approval.

### In-Store Counter Payments
- **Cash**: Accepted in USD at our Manhattan showroom counter.
- **Chip & PIN / NFC Contactless**: Supported on all registered debit and credit cards.

## 2. Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) & Financing Plans

ComputeIQ Tech partners with leading consumer financing providers to offer flexible monthly payment installments:

| Provider | Available Plans | Interest Rate | Minimum Order |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

## 3. Cryptocurrencies & Unaccepted Methods

- **Cryptocurrencies**: We currently **do not accept** Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, or other cryptocurrencies.
- **Personal Paper Checks**: We do not accept personal checks by mail or in-store. Cashier's checks and verified business corporate checks are accepted with prior manager authorization.

---

## 4. Payment Security & Encryption

- **PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliant**: All online transactions are processed through end-to-end tokenized payment gateways.
- **256-Bit SSL Encryption**: Every transaction is protected with military-grade TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **Zero Card Storage**: ComputeIQ Tech never stores raw credit card numbers, CVVs, or debit PIN codes on our servers or databases.
