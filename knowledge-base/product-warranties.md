## 2. What Is Covered Under Warranty

The warranty covers internal hardware malfunctions, component failures, and manufacturing defects occurring under normal, intended use:
- Motherboard and processor failures.
- Factory screen defects (including display flickering, line artifacts, and more than 3 dead pixels).
- Internal battery failures (e.g., failure to retain charge or battery health degrading below 80% within warranty period).
- Non-functional keyboards, touchpads, and factory port failures.
- Charger power delivery malfunction or internal component burnout.
- NovaBuds charging case failure or audio driver loss of output under normal volume levels.

---

## 3. What Is NOT Covered (Exclusions)

The warranty does not cover issues resulting from external hazards, customer accidents, or misuse:
1. **Accidental Physical Damage**: Cracked screens, dented chassis, broken hinges, drops, or crushed casing.
2. **Liquid Damage**: Spillages, submersion, immersion, or corrosion caused by liquids.
3. **Unauthorized Modification**: Products opened, serviced, or repaired by unauthorized third-party technicians.
4. **Cosmetic Wear and Tear**: Scratches, cosmetic fading, paint peeling, or minor scuffs that do not affect device functionality.
5. **Software & Malware**: Operating system corruption, third-party software incompatibilities, malware, or ransomware.
6. **Serial Number Tampering**: Removal, defacing, or alteration of the original serial number sticker.

1. **In-Person**: Visit the ComputeIQ Tech Manhattan Showroom & Service Lounge (450 Lexington Ave, Suite 1200, New York, NY) with the product and original purchase receipt or email confirmation.
2. **Online RMA (Mail-in)**:
   - Email **support@computeiq.tech** with the subject `Warranty Claim - [Order Number]`.
   - Provide a description of the defect and a short video or photograph illustrating the issue.
   - If approved, ComputeIQ Tech issues a prepaid return shipping label.
   - Standard turnaround time for diagnostic inspection and repair is 3 to 5 business days.
